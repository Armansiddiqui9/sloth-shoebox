Onboarding asks, then downloads the right model.

Before the ledger opens, the app detects your RAM, recommends a vision model, and pulls it through Ollama. You pick a category or let the model suggest. Nothing leaves the machine.

1. How much RAM?

We read your system and place you in one of four bands: 8 GB, 12–16 GB, 16–19 GB+, or 20 GB+. This decides the model size.

2. Pick a model

Low — Moondream / MiniCPM-V. Medium — Gemma 4 12B. High — Qwen3.8-Vision 27B (4-bit). Very high — full precision Qwen3.8.

3. Download via Ollama

The app runs ollama pull for the recommended weight. After that, parsing is fully offline.

4. Start the ledger

Drop screenshots or open a folder. The pipeline dedupes, parses with your local VLM, validates against schema, and commits.

Model selection by your RAM.

The Windows app aligns with the web app’s degradation ladder. Smaller machines get slower but functional models; larger machines unlock the primary vision model.
